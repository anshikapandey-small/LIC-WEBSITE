About Your Advisor

Advice built on plans, not just policies

Over the last twelve years, I've worked with families across Pune to put a financial safety net under their biggest goals — a child's education, a comfortable retirement, or simply the peace of mind that comes from knowing your loved ones are protected.

My approach starts with your life stage and income, not a product catalogue. Every recommendation is backed by a written illustration, an honest comparison of alternatives, and no pressure to decide on the spot.
